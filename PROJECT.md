## Phase One: Plan

- List what is due. (Automatically mark everything as :red: (unfinished))

- When you click each one of them, mark it as :green: (done).

- You may also :yellow: (in progress. Auto = mark the immediate next one as in progress) or :white: (hung, skip this) a task.

## Phase Two: Visualize

- Keep track. Spread the entire workload over the work hours. Connect the task start & end nodes with a broken line.

- On breaks, the line flats on. After each task is finished, propose a 10-minute break. Taking breaks is productive.

- AI estimates ETA at the beginning, human reviews it. Progress bar. Prompt self-evaluation towards 50%.

- Overwork area beneath the line tinted red. The longer overwork hours are, the more red the screen becomes.
  - <30 min: blue #aed8fc (color change immediately after work hours finish)
  - 30 - 60 min: shifts towards red #db9696
  - 60 min - 120 min: more red
  - 120 min - : Stays in the darkest red #803d3a

## Phase Three: Summarize

- Produce work health status report @day, week & past 30 days

- AI pinpoint issues based on task content and execution progress, generates suggestions based on productive work principles.
